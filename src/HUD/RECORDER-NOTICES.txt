Bait & Tackle Incident Recorder

Engine: OBS Studio 33.0.1, GPL-2.0-or-later, https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio
Official release: https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/releases/tag/33.0.1
Portable archive SHA256: 0d9436451c2d772b33428e3fdae9776f071895c335de17283176306fb72651d3
Engine license and third-party notices are included in its data folder.

Ctrl+H / Ctrl+J -> Recorder: start/stop, save incident, select saved-clip folder.
Ctrl+Shift+F9 saves the current replay. No automatic upload or streaming.

The rolling replay is retained in OBS memory for up to 300 seconds; older packets
are discarded continuously. There is no ongoing disk recording and no deletion
of saved incidents. Five minutes at 3000 kbps video + 96 kbps audio is about
116 MB before container overhead. Buffer length can differ slightly at keyframes.
Native client resolution is captured up to 1920x1200 at 15 FPS. H.264 hardware
encoding is selected for NVIDIA/AMD/Intel where supported; otherwise x264 is used.

Only window_capture via Windows Graphics Capture, with EQ process audio, is
created. No game injection, microphone, desktop, browser, or streaming source.
OBS WebSocket is disabled; control uses a private local file-based Lua bridge.
The engine has its own portable config. A Windows job owns it and its child
muxers; closing the BFT helper or EQ stops them. Saved clips are MKV so a normal
completed save does not depend on writing an MP4 footer at exit.

Saved incidents are never automatically deleted. Stopping or closing EQ discards
the unsaved replay. Helper refresh also resets the current unsaved replay.

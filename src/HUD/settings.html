Warrior Defensive

Warriors level 55+ get a pinned Defensive reminder in Class Alerts. Observed activation starts a 3:00 countdown, with 30-second and 10-second ending warnings. Enable Class Alerts sound for spoken warnings. Reuse is estimated by level (10:00 at 60); other disciplines can alter availability. After helper restart or character change, readiness is unknown until a new activation is observed. Run /who to establish class and level. Reminders never activate a discipline for you.
